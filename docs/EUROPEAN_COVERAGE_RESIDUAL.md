# European expansion: coverage vs executable readiness

Universe: `src/european_coverage.py` lists European domestic top flights and UEFA Nations League, EURO Qualifying, European Qualifiers and club competitions. Registry membership is **not** evidence that a provider supplies an event, a fresh 1X2 price, a point-in-time lineup, or a settlement.

`dashboard/live_feed.py` checks the live authenticated Odds API sports catalog and activates only provider-verified keys. Exact-title matching may miss differently named competitions; manually review mapping, do not silently guess. `src/paper_portfolio.py` accepts the expanded competition names. `src/train_residual_alpha.py` accepts new factor names and reports coverage by competition; missing features remain missing. Current 300-match generator uses 1X2 only and does not claim that national-team SGP or all top flights are bookable.

**League-aware residual design**: `src/league_residual_features.py` stores candidate features and testable league hypotheses, not trained coefficient adjustments. Train global baseline plus group/league effects with hierarchical shrinkage once enough PIT rows exist; small leagues must borrow statistical strength. Compare out-of-time and leave-one-league-out performance and report group-specific calibration, ROI and book coverage. Never claim a blanket national league stereotype as a coefficient.

**National teams**: separate national-team from club team IDs, training data and competition format epochs. Use PIT squad callups, withdrawals, minutes played together, opponent-adjusted national team strength, travel, home/neutral, stage and group standings. For 2026/27 Nations League use its actual four-league format; UEFA announced a transition from 2028/29 to three leagues, which must not leak backward into old seasons. EURO qualifying format must be tied to edition and rules at entry. Do not train national-team weights from club-team goals.

**Execution blockers**: prior `data/sportsbook_consensus.json` said NO_ODDS_API_KEY. An Odds API key and actual coverage are required. Existing ESPN scoreboard supports only a limited set of leagues; newly added leagues/national matches need verified fixture-to-odds IDs and result feeds before they can settle. Keep unresolved tickets OPEN rather than guessing scores. The 300-match target counts unique fixtures, not tickets. Same-game parlays still require a real joint quote and correlated probability method.
